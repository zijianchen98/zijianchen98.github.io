Zijian Chen 「陈子健」

I am currently a third-year PhD Student of Multimedia Lab at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), advised by Prof. Guangtao Zhai and Prof. Wenjun Zhang. Previously, I received my M.E. degree from East China University of Science and Technology in June 2023, and B.S. degree in EE from Wenzhou University in June 2020.

I'm generally interested in Visual Quality Assessment, Agentic AI, especially All-round Evaluation, and Digital Humanities (Oracle bone character processing). My ultimate goal is to explore the limits of AI's capabilities.

I'm always eager to communicate and cooperate, so feel free to contact me!!!
