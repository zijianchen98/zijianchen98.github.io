<p> In this paper, we introduce <strong>CSMBench</strong>, a dataset comprising 1,041 highquality figures curated from premier journals up to September 2025. CSMBench categorizes data into four scientifically distinct regimes: atomic, micro, meso, and macro scales, strictly aligning with the focus and definitions in materials study.</p>
