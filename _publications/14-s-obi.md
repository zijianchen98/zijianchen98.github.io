<p> We introduce <strong>S-OBI</strong>, a novel benchmark for evaluating MLLMs in Sentence-level OBI understanding. S-OBI synthesizes standardized sentence-level OBI instances through glyph substitution and composition, consisting of semantic matching,
semantic slot extraction, and contextual reasoning tasks.</p>
